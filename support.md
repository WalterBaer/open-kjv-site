# Open KJV Support

Open KJV is a free King James Bible for iPhone and iPad. If something isn't working, or you have an idea, we'd like to hear it.

## Report a problem or share an idea

- **In the app:** shake your iPhone or iPad, or go to **More › Send feedback**. A screenshot and the details of where you were go with your note, so it's easy to see what happened. Choose **Send…** and pick Mail or Messages.
- **Here:** [open an issue](https://github.com/WalterBaer/open-kjv-site/issues/new) on GitHub.

## Common questions

**Does Open KJV need an internet connection?**
No. The Bible text, commentaries, confessions, pictures, music and reading voices are all in the app.

**Where are my highlights and notes kept?**
On your device only. To keep a copy or move to a new device, use **More › Backup**, then restore it on the new device.

**The reading voice takes a moment to start.**
The first time you listen, the on-device voice loads its pronunciations, which takes a few seconds. After that it starts at once.

**How do I cast to a TV?**
Use Screen Mirroring or AirPlay from Control Center while listening. The TV shows the chapter's engraving and the verses, following along. Play and pause from your iPhone, iPad or the lock screen.

**Why can't I see pictures of Christ?**
They are off by default, since many readers avoid them. Turn them on in **More › Reading settings › Book openings and engravings › Allow images of Christ**.

**Memorize can't hear me.**
Allow the microphone and speech recognition for Open KJV in the Settings app. Recognition happens on your device; if your device can't do that offline, you can type the passage instead.
