The King James Bible

Open KJV

The Authorized Version, set like a well-made book, with the old helps for reading and study close at hand. Free, with no ads, no account and no tracking.

Coming soon to the App Store · iPhone and iPad

Read

The 1769 Oxford text, checked word for word against the Cambridge and Oxford editions, with the translators' italics, the words of Christ in red and Doré engravings at each book.

Listen

Natural voices read aloud on your device, each verse lit as it is read, with Bach or soft rain beneath if you like. Keeps reading with the screen locked, and shows on your TV.

Study

Cross references, Matthew Henry, Spurgeon's Morning and Evening, Easton's Dictionary, and where each verse is cited in the creeds and catechisms.

The Confessions

The Westminster Standards, the 1689 Baptist Confession, the Three Forms of Unity, the ancient creeds, the 1650 Psalter and The Pilgrim's Progress.

Every day

A verse, a catechism question and your reading plan. No streaks and no nagging: missed days simply wait for you.

Memorize

Learn a verse or a whole passage, then say it aloud and see any words you missed. Speech is recognized on your device and never kept.
