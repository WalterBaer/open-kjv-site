# Open KJV Privacy Policy

*Effective October 17, 2026*

Open KJV is a free King James Bible app for iPhone and iPad. It is built to keep your reading private: **Open KJV does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any information about you.** There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## What stays on your device

Everything you make in the app stays on your iPhone or iPad:

- highlights, notes, prayers and bookmarks
- reading plans and reading progress
- memory verses and how you have done reciting them
- your settings
- feedback reports you choose to keep

None of this is sent anywhere by the app. It is removed if you delete the app. You can make a backup in **More › Backup**; a backup is a file or text that you save or send yourself.

The Bible text, commentaries, confessions, pictures, music and reading voices are all included in the app, so it works without an internet connection and makes no network requests while you use it.

## Microphone and speech recognition

When you choose to recite a passage in **Memorize**, the app asks to use the microphone and speech recognition. Your voice is turned into text **on your device** so the app can show which words you missed. The app requires on-device recognition, does not record or keep your audio, and does not send it to Apple or anyone else. The microphone is used only while you are reciting.

## Widgets

Open KJV's widgets show the verse of the day, your reading and a catechism question. The app passes this to the widgets on your device only.

## Feedback

If you send feedback, you choose how (for example Mail or Messages) in the share sheet, and it goes only to whoever you send it to. A report includes the text you write, a screenshot of where you were in the app and basic details such as your device model and iOS version, all shown to you before you send.

## Links

Some links in the app, such as sources on the About page or the page for supporting Open KJV, open in your web browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

## Children

Open KJV collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
